## ADVANCED TIPS FOR HEYGEN

**Tip 1 — Use "Talking Photo" for testimonials**  
Upload a real participant's photo and use HeyGen's Talking Photo feature to
animate it with a quote. This adds credibility without needing video footage.

**Tip 2 — Clone your own voice**  
If you have 30+ seconds of your own voice recording, use HeyGen Voice Clone
to make the avatar sound like you personally.

**Tip 3 — A/B test two versions**  
Create Version A with an urgent hook ("Are your videos being ignored?") and
Version B with an aspirational hook ("Imagine your videos getting 100K views").
Run both as ads and compare click-through rates.

**Tip 4 — Add subtitles**  
Enable HeyGen's auto-caption feature. 85% of social media video is watched
without sound. Captions keep viewers engaged and boost completion rates.

**Tip 5 — Brand consistency**  
Upload your brand kit (logo PNG with transparent background, brand colors hex
codes) into HeyGen's Brand Kit settings so every element stays on-brand.
