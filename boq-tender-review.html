1. Executive Summary

This report presents the Senior QS & Commercial Manager's tender review of BOQ Ref MC-BOQ-WH6080-001 (Rev A) for the Warehouse 60×80m project. The original BOQ totals $312,522.00 USD based on cost-estimate rates. Following a comprehensive review of quantities, market rates, risk exposure, and missing scope, the following adjustments have been applied:

• 10% Additional Rate Increase applied across all applicable items to cover material price escalation, logistics, and contingency.
• 35% Tender Markup applied to achieve target gross margin of 25–30% after overhead absorption.
• Missing scope items identified and priced (approx. $18,500 additional).
• 8 underpriced items flagged and corrected to market rates.

Commercial Manager's Recommendation

Recommended Tender Submission Value: $489,070 USD (excl. steel structure & roof)

This represents a 35% markup on the revised direct cost, yielding an estimated gross margin of 26–28% after overhead absorption. The tender remains competitive within the Cambodia industrial warehouse market range of $95–115/m² for civil & structural works (this tender: $101.9/m²).

Key conditions for submission:

  1. Include price escalation clause for concrete and steel (items exceeding 60 days from award).
  2. Confirm soil investigation report — tender is conditional on 6m pile length assumption.
  3. Laterite sub-base by Owner — include EOT provision for late supply.
  4. Lock rebar supply price within 14 days of contract award.
  5. Provisional sum of $5,000 recommended for extra pile length (if ground conditions differ).
  6. Validity period: 90 days from submission date.
